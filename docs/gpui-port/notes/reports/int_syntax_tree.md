### Move Statement, case by case (native, through the action)

- **`ideaParity.test.ts` (JavaScript):**
  - "moves a multi-line statement as a whole": same.
  - "moves into a block past its header and out of it past its closing brace, re-indented": **different**. The first press passes the whole `if` (`  if (x) {\n    b();\n  }\n  |a();`) where the webview moves into the block (`  if (x) {\n    |a();`). The three-press end text matches only by coincidence. Not fixed: it needs the webview's own planner over a whole tree, which the backend does not provide.
  - "never carries a statement out of its function": same.
  - "passes a neighbouring function as a whole": same.
- **`structural.test.ts` (Kotlin, real backend ranges; the sibling-sharing case in Haskell):** all same. That is: swaps with the next statement; multi-line statement up; member with its comment; first/last statement (both ways); member with errors inside it, and one broken at its header (moves as a line); sibling sharing lines; fallback for a shared line; asks at the caret line start; drops a stale answer.
- No other test in `src/` or `e2e/` uses `moveStatement`.
