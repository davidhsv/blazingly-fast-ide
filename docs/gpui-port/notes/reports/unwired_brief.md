## What this is

Parity is tracked row by row in `docs/gpui-port/parity.json` (key → `{status, proof}`; a proof cites native tests by full name or module in backticks) and `docs/gpui-port/INVENTORY.md` (read its top: a row at parity means the running app does it, proven by a test; "model only" means tested logic that no window reaches).
