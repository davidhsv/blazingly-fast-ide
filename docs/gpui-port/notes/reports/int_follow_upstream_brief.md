## Rules for this slice

- The webview at HEAD is the reference; the merge reports are leads. Where the fork has its own behaviour on top (Blaze and Deep modes, text-based Find Usages: `git log --oneline 37c2a604..a950eeca`), keep it.
- No existing assertion is loosened; an expectation that changes is explained by the upstream commit and the webview's `file:line`. A test is removed only when the thing it tested is gone from the webview.
